# Summary - Villain Contending for Punt of the Week
This guy heard about Punt of the Week and tried to qualify! Good effort, chief. I am happy to take your stack; you clearly did not want it anymore.


### Hand Summary: $0.02/$0.05 No Limit Hold'em
- **Hero (UTG):** $Kd$ $Kc$ ($1.52 stack)
- **Villain (UTG+1):** $Kh$ $Tc$ ($4.48 stack)
- **Board:** $9c$ $4h$ $4s$ $Jh$ $3d$
- **Result:** Hero wins **$2.96** with Kings and Fours (Two Pair).

# Breakdown
**Preflop:**
- Hero raises to **$0.20** from UTG. UTG+1 calls. All others fold.
- _Pot: $0.47_

**Flop ($9c$ $4h$ $4s$):**
- Hero bets a tiny **$0.05**. UTG+1 raises to **$0.58**. Hero calls.
- _Pot: $1.63_

**Turn ($Jh$):**
- Hero checks. UTG+1 moves **All-In**. Hero calls for his remaining **$0.74**.
- _Pot: $3.11_

**River ($3d$):**
- Board finishes with no help for the Villain.

# Breakdown
## Preflop
Larger size than I typically choose, but this was a splashy table. Happy to take advantage of that with a premium hand.

## Flop
I am not sure why I min bet here, but it ended up working as well as it possibly could have. We trapped him into a big raise here and eventually giving us his whole stack. Maybe he had some sort of read on me and thought I was full of it. Idk. Even so, KTo air is maybe not the hand with which to exploit that read? I have a no brainer call when he raises pretty big to an overbet.

## Turn
The jack is a safe card. It brings some backdoor straight draws into play, but few if any of those remain in his range after his flop bet. He is repping 4x, 99, and air. Maybe JJ could have overbet that flop and hit the dream turn? He shoves this turn which lends credibility to his value hands, but he is pot committed at this point and has a lot of bluffs in his range. We have to call with kings. 

## River
River changes nothing. What in the world was this guy thinking! Thanks for the stack mate lets do it again sometime.

## Takeaway
I don't usually use these trappy min bets. Perhaps I should try to find some more spots like this for them, especially against opponents who are prone to respond the way this guy did.

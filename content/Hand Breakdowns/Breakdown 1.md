# Summary - Full House vs Runner Runner Quads
This was a hilarious bad beat. Feels appropriate for the first breakdown. I botched the flop, turned into a massive favorite, and lost to a one outer on the river. Lol

- **Stakes:** $0.02 / $0.05 NL Hold'em
- **Hero (BB):** Q♠Q♡
- **Small Blind:** K♢J♠
- **Result:** Small Blind wins **$2.78** with **Four of a Kind (Jacks)**.

# Action

**Pre-Flop**
- Folds to SB
- **Small Blind (SB)** raises to $0.10.
- **Hero (BB)** 3-bets to $0.25 with Pocket Queens.
- SB calls.
**Flop ($0.50 Pot)**
- **Board:** 8♡Q♢J♣
- Hero flops **Top Set** (Queens).
- SB checks, Hero bets $0.05, and SB calls.
**Turn ($0.60 Pot)**
- **Board:** 8♡Q♢J♣J♢
- Hero improves to **Full House** (Queens full of Jacks).
- SB checks. Hero bets $0.50.
- SB raises **All-In** for $1.11.
- Hero calls
**River ($2.92 Pot)**
- **Board:** 8♡Q♢J♣J♢J♡
- The final Jack hits the board, giving SB **Four of a Kind**.
### **Final Hands**
- **Small Blind:** J♠J♣J♢J♡K♢ (Four of a Kind, Jacks)
- **Hero:** Q♠Q♡Q♢J♣J♢ (Full House, Queens full of Jacks)

# Breakdown

## Preflop 
Pretty standard. Folds to SB and he min raises with KJo. With a premium hand like QQ in BB this is a 3bet for pure value. 

## Flop
SB checks to me and I accidentally click min bet. Whoops. Did not want to do that. With top set on a rainbow board I am tempted to slow play here and check back, but the better move is probably to raise 30-50%. He has more straight and straight draw combos in his range than me. We can extract value from those combos, any Jx, some pocket pairs, and maybe some 8x while denying equity. 

He calls a min bet here, which in my opinion is equivalent to a check, so we can be pretty confident that he did not flop a straight or a set. This becomes irrelevant on the turn. 

## Turn
We fill up on the turn--beautiful! He checks and I go for a standard large turn size 83%--pure value with the second nuts. He shoves and we have a no brainer call with a boat. 

When he check shoves, I figure he has QJ and I win--bad beat, tough one SB. Maybe I lose to slowplayed JJ that turned quads? Doesn't seem likely--he has been too passive. Maybe he is overplaying trips or a draw--entirely possible especially given my weird size on the flop--and I have him dominated. Regardless, no brainer call here with a boat. 

## River
I seem him turn over trips and I am already counting my winnings when... he hits runner runner quads?? Nice! The only card in the deck that beats us. Cool cool cool... nice hand chief. 

## Takeaway
I misclicked on the flop. I doubt it would have changed anything--I don't think he is folding middle pair with an overcard to any appropriately sized value bet. I wouldn't do anything differently on the other streets. Just need to patch the hole in my drywall I certainly didn't create by bashing my head through it after this one.

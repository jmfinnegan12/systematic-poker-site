- **Game:** $0.02/$0.05 No Limit Hold'em (Ignition)
- **Hero (BB):** $Kd$ $8s$
- **Villain (SB):** $Th$ $Td$
- **Board:** $7h$ $7s$ $2h$ $6s$ $9h$
- **Result:** Villain wins a **$6.38 pot** (SB's Tens over Sevens beats Hero's Sevens with King kicker).

# Action
## Pre-Flop
- Folds to BTN. BTN limps
- SB raises to 4x $0.20
- Hero (BB) calls with K8o
- Dealer calls

## Flop ($0.60)
- Board: $7h$ $7s$ $2h$
- Hero airballs this flop
- SB checks. **Hero bets $0.43** (approx. 70% pot). Dealer folds; SB calls.

## Turn ($1.46)
- Board: $7h$ $7s$ $2h$ $6s$
- Hero airballs this turn
- SB checks again. **Hero bets $1.50** (overbet). SB calls.

## River ($4.46):
-  Board: $7h$ $7s$ $2h$ $6s$ $9h$
- Hero airballs this river
- SB checks a third time. **Hero moves All-In for $0.96**. SB calls.

# Breakdown
## Preflop
K8o is a fold in the BB multiway here. This hand plays poorly postflop and is often dominated. We want to the big blind but stick to two way pots and/or better hands

## Flop
I took a shot here. BTN limp called I didn't expect him to stick around. This flop favors my range over SB. Yolo.

I should have backed off big time when SB calls 70% on the flop. 

## Turn
But we persist! Keep telling the story! This card again favors my range and now we have two flush draws, so I rip an overbet. 

In hindsight there is no way SB folds to a 20% river shove, so this turn bet committed my stack and left me with hardly any ammo. I should be be check folding here--simple as that. But the oversight is compounded not only by the decision to bet (wrong) but also by handcuffing myself with bad sizing. 

He calls and I feel sick to my stomach.

## River
Heart flush hits. I whiff. All great things must come full circle, the sun must rise in the east every day, I am destined to complete this punt. I shove for 21.5% pot and SURE ENOUGH, SB calls with an overpair because I gave him a fantastic price!

## Takeaway
If I was SB I probably would have folded the tens to the turn overbet--scary board with a pair and two flush draws, but he was smart. My line was extremely bluffy from the start. Following up a preflop call with big barrels screams bluff, not value. 

Looking more closely at our ranges. I have a few weak 7x combos like 97s, 87s, 76s, maybe some offsuit 7x. BB probably 3bets strong 7x combos like 77 and A7s there a lot of the time, but SB has all of these combos in his range. So my value combos are pretty nonexistent: a few 7x, some weak flush draws, and 22!

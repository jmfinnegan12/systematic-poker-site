# Attempt to Steal % (ATS)
## Formula
$$ATS = \frac{\text{Raises from steal positions (CO/BTN/SB) when folded to}}{\text{Steal opportunities}}*100$$
## What it tells you
How often a player raises in late position when action folds to them preflop. Measures willingness to exploit positional advantage and attack the blinds.

## Optimal Range
6max: 30-50% (varies by position)

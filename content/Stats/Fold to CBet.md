# Fold to CBet %
## Formula
$$\text{Fold to CBet}\% = \frac{\text{Folds to continuation bets}}{\text{Times faced continuation bet}}*100$$
## What it tells you
How often a player surrenders when facing a continuation bet. High values signal exploitable passivity; low values suggest over-calling or floating too often.

## Optimal Range
6max: 35-55%

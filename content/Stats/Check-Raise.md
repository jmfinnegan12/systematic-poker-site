# Check-Raise %
## Formula
$$\text{Check-Raise}\% = \frac{\text{Check-raises}}{\text{Times checked with opportunity to check-raise}}*100$$
## What it tells you
How often a player check-raises rather than calls or folds when checked. Reflects deceptive postflop aggression and bluffing frequency.

## Optimal Range
6max: 8-15%

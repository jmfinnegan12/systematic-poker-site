# Fold to BB Steal %
## Formula
$$\text{Fold to BB Steal}\% = \frac{\text{BB folds to steal attempt}}{\text{Steal attempts vs BB}}*100$$
## What it tells you
How often the big blind folds to a late-position steal raise. High values indicate an exploitable over-folding tendency from the big blind.

## Optimal Range
6max: 55-70%

# Fold to 3-Bet %
## Formula
$$\text{Fold to 3-Bet}\% = \frac{\text{Hands folded to 3-bet}}{\text{Hands facing 3-bet}}*100$$
## What it tells you
How often a player folds their open-raise when faced with a 3-bet. High values indicate exploitable passivity; low values indicate over-defending preflop.

## Optimal Range
6max: 50-65%

# Big Blinds per 100 Hands (BB/100)
## Formula
$$BB/100 = \frac{\text{Net profit (in big blinds)}}{\text{Total hands}}*100$$
## What it tells you
The primary measure of overall win rate and profitability. Normalizes results across stakes and session lengths by expressing profit in big blinds rather than dollar amounts.

## Optimal Range
6max: positive (5+ BB/100 indicates a solid winning player)

# Won When Saw Flop % (WWSF)
## Formula
$$WWSF = \frac{\text{Pots won after seeing flop}}{\text{Times saw flop}}*100$$
## What it tells you
How often a player wins the pot once they've seen the flop. Captures both showdown and non-showdown wins and reflects overall postflop effectiveness.

## Optimal Range
6max: 42-50%
